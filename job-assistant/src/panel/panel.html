    Verified profile

    Only these routine fields are ever filled. Sensitive questions (demographics, salary, work authorization, disability, veteran status, legal attestations, and similar) are always left for you.

    Resume versions

      Drafts wait here for your approval. The assistant never sends connection requests or messages. After you approve one, it can open the profile and copy the text; you paste and click Send yourself. LinkedIn limits how many invitations you can send, and its rules prohibit automating them.

          How this assistant works

          • Runs only in your own signed-in Chrome. It uses no proxies, no other accounts, no copied cookies, and nothing that hides that you are using an extension.
          • Works only on jobs you add or import yourself, one application at a time.
          • Fills only routine fields from your verified profile, only when you click Fill routine fields, and never overwrites what you typed.
          • Leaves every sensitive, unusual, or unclear question for you and outlines it in orange or red.
          • Never clicks Submit. You make the final Submit click on every application.
          • Queues LinkedIn connection requests and messages for your approval. Even after approval you send them yourself.
          • Stops right away and labels the job Manual action required if it sees a CAPTCHA, a security or verification check, an account warning, a sign-in prompt, or a page it doesn't recognise. It does not try to get around any of these.
          • Keeps an activity log of every job, resume version, field filled, document attached, and submission status.
